🏏 ROHIT SHARMA 🏏

The Hitman of Indian Cricket

Indian Captain | World-Class Opener | Record Breaker

📖 Introduction

Rohit Sharma is one of the most successful and stylish batsmen in world cricket. Known for his elegant stroke play and incredible ability to score big centuries, he has become one of India's greatest cricket legends. Millions of fans around the world admire him for his calm leadership, explosive batting, and dedication to the game.

Rohit is the only player in the history of One Day Internationals (ODIs) to score three double centuries. His highest score of 264 remains the highest individual score ever recorded in ODI cricket.
